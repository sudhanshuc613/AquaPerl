🛡️ FAKE / SPAM CALLS SE BACHAO (Extra Tips)
================================================================

1. Ads sirf 8 AM to 9 PM chalao — is time mein 90% real customers
   hote hain. Raat ke calls mostly spam ya wrong number hote hain.

2. Minimum call duration 30 seconds set karo — robocalls, miss call
   fraud wale 2-5 second mein cut karte hain.

3. Auto-call attend mat lagao (IVR). Real aadmi phone uthao, "Hello
   AquaMedii RO Service, main aapse kaise madad kar sakta hoon?" —
   is se bhi spam calls ka pata chalta hai.

4. Negative keywords aggressively add karo — yeh #1 tareeka hai
   waste calls rokne ka.

5. "Search partners" OFF (tumne bola tha, maine already upar mention
   kiya). Ye sabse bada fake traffic source hai.

6. Agar koi "agency" ya "Google se bol raha hoon" wala call aaye,
   turant block karo woh number.
