STEP 0: PEHLE EK BAAT YAAD RAKHO
================================================================
Google Ads kholte hi tumhe "Smart Campaign" ya "Get more calls"
jaisa easy option dikhayega — USS MAT JAANA. Woh Google ka
fool-proof option hai jo tumhara paisa zyada udata hai aur calls
kam deta hai. Hum EXPERT MODE mein kaam karenge.

Agar koi bhi screen pe aage badhne se pehle "Switch to Expert Mode"
ya "Expert Mode" dikhe, turant us par click kar lena.

================================================================
STEP 1: GOOGLE ADS ACCOUNT BANAO (shuru mein)
================================================================

1. Computer pe Chrome browser kholo.
2. Address bar mein type karo: https://ads.google.com
3. Upar right side "Sign in" button pe click karo.
4. Apna wohi Gmail ID login karo jo tumhari GMB (Google Business
   Profile) se connected hai. Yeh zaroori hai — isse GBP reviews
   aur location ad mein automatically aa jayenge.
5. Login ke baad "New Google Ads account" screen ayega.
   - Agar "What's your main advertising goal?" dikhe to NICHE
     ek chhota link hoga: "Switch to Expert Mode" — us par click
     kar do.
   - Agar "Choose a campaign type" seedha aa jaye to matlab expert
     mode mein ho, aage badho.

================================================================
STEP 2: BILLING SETUP (pehli baar karna hai)
================================================================

1. Upar right side pe Tools (wrench icon 🛠) par click karo.
2. "Billing" section mein "Settings" par click karo.
3. Ya directly "Payments" option hoga — wahan click karo.
4. Details bharo:
   - Country: India
   - Time zone: (GMT+05:30) India Standard Time — Asia/Kolkata
   - Currency: Indian Rupee (INR)
     ⚠️ Currency ek baar set ho gaya to badal nahi sakta! INR hi
     select karna.
   - Account type: Business
   - Business name: AquaMedii - RO Service Patna

   - Tax info: GST agar hai to daalo, nahi to chhor do (optional)
   - Payment method: Manual payments select karo (automatic payments

   PEHLA TOP-UP sirf ₹1000 karo. (Poora ₹5000 mat dalo pehle,
   7 din test karna hai.)

STEP 8: CONVERSION TRACKING SETUP (calls ko count karna)
================================================================

Yeh step optional hai lekin karna hi chahiye.

1. Upar right side Tools 🛠 icon par click → "Conversions"
   (Measurement section mein).
2. Blue "+ New conversion action" button.
3. Select karo "Phone calls"
4. 3 option mein se "Calls from ads" select karo, "Continue".
5. Ab "Phone call conversions" mein settings:
   - Conversion name: "Ad Call (30+ sec)"
   - Value: "Don't use a value for this conversion action" chuno,
     ya ₹1000 maan ke daal do (ek call ki approx value).
   - Count: "One" (matlab ek call = ek conversion, har redial
     ko count na kare)
   - Call length: Set karo 30 seconds (matlab agar call 30 sec
     se zyada chala to genuine customer tha, chhote calls spam)
   - Click-through conversion window: 30 days
   - Include in "Conversions": YES
   - Attribution model: Last click
6. Save / "Create and continue" dabao.

Ab Google aapke ads se aane wali 30-second se zyada ki calls ko
"conversion" maanta hai, aur future mein bidding ko iske hisaab se
optimize karega.

STEP 10: AD APPROVAL (1-24 HOURS)
================================================================

Ab tumhare ads Google ke review mein chala jayenge. Usually 1-3
ghante mein approve ho jate hain, kabhi kabhi 24 lagte hain.

"Ads and assets" → "Ads" page pe jaake dekh sakte ho status:
- "Pending review" = abhi review ho raha hai
- "Eligible" / "Approved" = chalu ho gaya hai
- "Disapproved" = ad mein kuch galat hai — edit karke theek karo,
  usually special characters, capital letters zyada, ya "best"
  "number 1" jaise words — unko hata ke resubmit karo.

   apne paise kat jayenge. Sirf dekh ke confirm karo.
6. Din mein ek do baar alag keywords se check karo.

STEP 12: PEHLE 10 DIN DAILY KYA KARNA HAI (5 MINUTE)
================================================================

Har din subah ya shaam 5 minute:

1. Google Ads dashboard login.
2. Left menu "Keywords" → "Search terms" par click karo.
3. Wahan list dikhegi ki log aaj kya-kya search karke tumhare
   ad pe aaye.
4. Jo bhi irrelevant lage (job, wholesale, youtube, amazon, dusre
   city, customer care number, review, complaint etc.) uske
   saamne wale checkbox ko tick karo, aur "Add as negative
   keyword" button dabao.
5. Campaign 1 aur Campaign 2 dono ke liye karo.
6. "Overview" page pe dekh lo kitne clicks aaye, kitni calls aayi.
7. Agar koi ad disapproved dikhe to theek karo.

⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES JO MAT KARNA
================================================================

1. ❌ Apne aap ad pe click karna (paise barbaad)
2. ❌ Broad match keywords use karna (uncontrolled traffic)
3. ❌ Search partners ON rakhna (fake traffic)
4. ❌ Raat 12 baje tak ad chala ke phone off karna (missed calls)
5. ❌ Pehle din hi ₹5000 ka top-up kar dena (bina test ke)
6. ❌ Negative keywords nahi daalna (50% waste)
7. ❌ Google ke "Smart Campaign" suggestion pe click kar dena
8. ❌ Display network pe ads chala dena (zero calls, sare clicks
   fake websites se)
9. ❌ Ad mein phone number alag dalna (GBP aur website se alag)
10. ❌ Ad copy mein "Best", "No.1", "Top 1" jaisi superlative words
    mat likho (Google disapprove kar deta hai)
